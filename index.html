WHAT GETS PATCHED

  • HOLES

    Open edges are closed flat where they can be, and with a fan where they can't. Zero-width slivers are stitched into the face next to them.

  • INSIDE WALLS

    Paper-thin walls left where parts were joined are taken out, so every edge is shared by exactly two faces.

  • FLIPPED FACES

    Faces are turned to point outward, and cavities inward, so the slicer knows what's solid.

  • SPECKS

    Flat or tiny loose bits that can't print are removed. The real parts are kept.

FAQ

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. The repair runs in a worker inside this browser tab. Your STL is read from your disk and the fixed copy is handed straight back to you.

Will it change the shape of my model?

It shouldn't. Hullpatch only removes faces that can't be part of a printable solid and fills holes. If a fix would change the model's volume, it is rolled back and that part is left as it was.

Does it work with Bambu Studio?

Yes. Repaired files are checked against the same rules Bambu Studio uses: no open edges, no non-manifold edges and no reversed faces.

What if my file is too broken?

Each repair stops after 4 minutes so your browser stays responsive. A model that needs longer is usually far from a closed shape, and is better fixed by hand in your CAD tool.
