    WHAT GETS PATCHED

    • HOLES

      Open edges are closed flat where they can be, and with a fan where they can't. Zero-width slivers are stitched into the face next to them.

    • INSIDE WALLS

      Paper-thin walls left where parts were joined are taken out, so every edge is shared by exactly two faces.

    • FLIPPED FACES

      Faces are turned to point outward, and cavities inward, so the slicer knows what's solid.

    • SPECKS

      Flat or tiny loose bits that can't print are removed. The real parts are kept.

    FAQ

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The repair runs in a worker inside this browser tab, and the 3D view runs in the page. Your STL is read from your disk and the fixed copy is handed straight back to you. The only downloads are the site's own code.

    Will it change the shape of my model?

    Only where it has to. Holes are closed with new faces, inside walls and duplicate faces are taken out, and loose bits that are flat or under about 0.1% of the model's size are removed. Where three or more faces share an edge, that cleanup is undone if it would change the volume by more than 0.1%. When the repair finishes, the result says whether the volume changed and by how much.

    Does it work with Bambu Studio?

    That's what it's built for. After the repair, Hullpatch checks the file for the three problems Bambu Studio reports: open edges, non-manifold edges and flipped faces. If none are left it says "watertight"; if some couldn't be fixed safely it says "partly repaired". Test files that came out watertight also passed Bambu Studio's own check.

    What if my file is too broken?

    Files up to 150 MB are accepted, and each repair stops after 4 minutes so your browser stays responsive. If a repair is stopped or comes back "partly repaired", fix the model in your CAD tool or re-export it, then try again.
